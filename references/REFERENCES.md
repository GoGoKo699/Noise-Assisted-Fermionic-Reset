## Dephasing chains, transport, and absorption

### Alba25

Vincenzo Alba, *Free fermions with dephasing and boundary driving: Bethe Ansatz results*, **SciPost Physics Core 8**, 011 (2025).  
[Journal / DOI](https://doi.org/10.21468/SciPostPhysCore.8.1.011) · [arXiv:2309.12978v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.12978v2) · [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2309.12978v2).  
Relevant locations: Sec. 2, Eqs. (1)–(8), for the generator and closure; Secs. 3.5, 4, and 5.5 for the spectral and asymptotic density results.

### TS21

Xhek Turkeshi and Marco Schiró, *Diffusion and thermalization in a boundary-driven dephasing model*, **Physical Review B 104**, 144301 (2021).  
[Journal / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.104.144301) · [arXiv:2106.13180v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.13180v1) · [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2106.13180v1).  
Relevant locations: Secs. II–III for the model, correlation closure, resolvent, and stochastic interpretation; Sec. IV for stationary transport.

### Z15

Marko Žnidarič, *Relaxation times of dissipative many-body quantum systems*, **Physical Review E 92**, 042143 (2015).  
[Journal / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.92.042143) · [arXiv:1507.07773v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07773v2).  
Relevant locations: Sec. III.A, especially Eqs. (8)–(9), for the dephasing crossover; Sec. IV and Tables I–II for boundary-dissipation gap comparisons.

### MEP16

Mariya V. Medvedyeva, Fabian H. L. Essler, and Tomaž Prosen, *Exact Bethe Ansatz Spectrum of a Tight-Binding Chain with Dephasing Noise*, **Physical Review Letters 117**, 137202 (2016).  
[Journal / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.117.137202) · [arXiv:1606.09122](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.09122).  
Background for the dephasing model's exact spectral structure, not a complete-reset theorem.

### PH08

Martin B. Plenio and Susana F. Huelga, *Dephasing-assisted transport: quantum networks and biomolecules*, **New Journal of Physics 10**, 113019 (2008).  
[Journal / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/10/11/113019) · [arXiv:0807.4902v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.4902v1) · [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/0807.4902v1).  
Relevant locations: “The basic setting” and “Linear chain,” which fix the localized-input transfer task and its uniform-chain comparison.

### ZCBK17

Yang Zhang, G. Luca Celardo, Fausto Borgonovi, and Lev Kaplan, *Optimal dephasing for ballistic energy transfer in disordered linear chains*, **Physical Review E 96**, 052103 (2017).  
[Journal / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.96.052103) · [arXiv:1706.09524v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.09524v1).  
A prior analysis of useful dephasing scales for ballistic excitation transfer with disorder and a sink.

### R26

Francisco Riberi, *Boundary-localized non-Hermitian modes in an absorbing quantum walk*, **arXiv:2605.08056v4** (2026).  
[Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08056v4) · [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.08056v4).  
Relevant locations: Secs. 2–4 and Appendices A–D, including the exact half-line propagator and absorption probability. This is the current title of the preprint initially circulated as *An Exactly Solvable Absorbing Quantum Walk*.

## Decoherent mixing and correlation methods

### FST06

Leonid Fedichkin, Dmitry Solenov, and Christino Tamon, *Mixing and decoherence in continuous-time quantum walks on cycles*, **Quantum Information and Computation 6**, 263–276 (2006).  
[Journal / DOI](https://doi.org/10.26421/QIC6.3-3) · [arXiv:quant-ph/0509163v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0509163v1).  
Relevant locations: Secs. 3–5 for small, large, and intermediate decoherence.

### R07

Peter C. Richter, *Quantum speedup of classical mixing processes*, **Physical Review A 76**, 042306 (2007).  
[Journal / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.76.042306) · [arXiv:quant-ph/0609204v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0609204v2).  
Relevant locations: Secs. 3–4, including Corollary 4.3 on periodic lattices. The task is mixing of measured walk distributions.

### BZ22

Thomas Barthel and Yikang Zhang, *Solving quasi-free and quadratic Lindblad master equations for open fermionic and bosonic systems*, **Journal of Statistical Mechanics: Theory and Experiment 2022**, 113101 (2022).  
[Journal / DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1742-5468/ac8e5c) · [arXiv:2112.08344v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.08344v5).  
Background for quasi-free covariance dynamics and the distinction between Gaussian preservation and a closed correlation hierarchy with Hermitian quadratic jumps.
